## Result

The [authoring decision](../../design/QUESTION-AUTHORING-DECISION.md) selects **Tiptap OSS 3.32.0 + MathLive 0.111.1 + KaTeX 0.19.0** as the researched implementation baseline, with a custom editable equation node and bundled `mhchem` planned for chemistry/units. It distinguishes tested behavior from required future implementation and unverified native configurations. No runtime dependency or preview UI changed.

Required Word support uses reviewed paste plus a **separate DOCX conversion/repair workflow in 6.1.3**. Pandoc's document-aware OMML reader is the preferred engine to prove; no converter binary is admitted or installed yet. Exact-release security, fixture fidelity and GPL/distribution review are prerequisites. Source inventory, missing-content reconciliation, region mapping, bounded isolation and publication blocking prevent a converter's silent loss from becoming an accepted question.

Copy LaTeX and configuration-qualified MathML remain the outbound scope. Direct native clipboard and full Word visual fidelity remain unverified; full DOCX export is a separate decision. Unsupported PDF/images/OLE use retained originals and explicit manual repair or approved non-editable images, without default OCR or embedded-object execution.

Provisional authoring/conversion allowance is **7–12 workdays**, excluding the rest of quiz/attempt/grading work. It exceeds the original three-day Phase 6 timebox and must feed reforecasting. The six-week stretch target remains visible, with no silent scope reduction or guaranteed completion date.
